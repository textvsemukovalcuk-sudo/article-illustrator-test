# «Что появляется рядом?» — концептуальная сюжетная сцена

Use case: illustration-story. Conceptual editorial illustration for a reflective Russian-language personal essay.

LAYOUT: Wide unified realistic scene with a left-to-right emotional flow: damaged wheel at lower left, heroine in the center, practical resources forming an open supportive semicircle, and the available route into the tire shop at right. Do not turn the scene into an infographic or a collection of floating icons.

ZONES:
- PROBLEM ZONE: the cut, deflated tire remains small but unmistakable in a subdued corner; it is a real task, not a disaster.
- SUPPORT ZONE: around the heroine, naturally placed in the same environment, are the emergency triangle, compressor, spare wheel in the open trunk, attentive helper and free mechanic.
- ROUTE ZONE: the tire shop bay stands open with no queue, creating a clear unobstructed path toward the solution.

FOCAL POINT: the heroine noticing that the necessary resources already exist around the problem; express realization through posture and gaze.

LABELS: If and only if perfectly legible, include one short handwritten Russian question in open negative space: «Что появляется рядом?» No other text.

COLORS: cream #FFFAF0 and soft peach #FED7AA for the supportive field; warm orange #ED8936, golden yellow #F6AD55 and terracotta #C05621 to connect the practical resources; deep brown #744210 for stable anchors; muted warm charcoal #4A4A4A only around the damaged tire. Color names and hex values are guidance only and must not appear visibly.

STYLE: Warm human-centered editorial illustration with soft paper texture, rounded forms, gentle warm gradients and soft shadows.
