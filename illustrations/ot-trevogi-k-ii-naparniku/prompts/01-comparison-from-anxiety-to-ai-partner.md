# От тревоги — к ИИ-напарнику

Use case: infographic-diagram. Create a polished Russian-language comparison illustration for an educational article.

## Layout

Wide left-to-right transformation with three clearly separated zones and generous margins. The middle zone is a visual bridge, not a literal doorway.

## ZONES

- **LEFT — «Тревога»**: simplified stylized student sits before an empty AI chat field; tangled black lines, one question mark, compact labels «Пустая строка», «С чего начать?», «Сотни промптов?». Lavender card, uncertain posture, airy rather than dramatic.
- **CENTER — «Первый безопасный шаг»**: a calm mentor's simplified hand points to one short prompt in a chat bubble; a curved hand-drawn arrow moves left to right. Peach card. Show the action sequence through three tiny labels: «Здесь нажимаем» → «Сюда пишем» → «Попробуйте сами».
- **RIGHT — «ИИ-напарник»**: the same recognizable student stands confidently beside a friendly abstract AI assistant symbol; compact icons for plan, text, learning, and ideas. Mint card with the short label «Использую в жизни».

## LABELS

Render only these exact Russian phrases, legibly and without substitutions: «От тревоги — к ИИ-напарнику», «Тревога», «Пустая строка», «С чего начать?», «Сотни промптов?», «Первый безопасный шаг», «Здесь нажимаем», «Сюда пишем», «Попробуйте сами», «ИИ-напарник», «Использую в жизни».

## COLORS

Warm Cream paper background #F5F0E8. Deep Charcoal #2D2D2D for every outline, arrow, doodle, and text. Lavender #D5C6E0 for uncertainty, Peach #FFD5C2 for human guidance, Mint #B5E5CF for confidence and practical use, Sky Blue #A8D8EA for small technology icons. Coral Red #E8655A only underlines the word «Тревога».

## STYLE

Hand-drawn educational sketch-notes: slightly wobbly black ink, rounded pastel blocks with imperfect hand-painted fills, subtle matte paper grain, tiny stars and underlines used sparingly. Diagram-style only, no realism, no photography, no gradients, shadows, logos, brand interfaces, or watermark. Human figures are simplified stylized silhouettes with no detailed faces. All text is large, prominent, hand-lettered, and minimal.

## ASPECT

16:9 landscape, medium complexity, presentation-ready, generous whitespace.
